ASM-01 // Design → Fabrication → Testing
Featured case study // Integrated mechanical system

CLAW
MACHINE.

A five-person engineering project built around a physical three-axis machine. The system combined a 20 × 20 mm aluminum-extrusion structure, user-driven X-Y motion, a motorized vertical axis, a pneumatic claw, 3D-printed PETG-CF brackets, and clear plexiglass into one working prototype.

My contribution

Led construction of the frame and gantry base with emphasis on structural stability and mounting precision, and assisted with pneumatic installation and tubing. The complete machine was developed collaboratively by the five-person team.

01 // STRUCTURE

Frame

20 × 20 mm aluminum extrusion, PETG-CF printed brackets, and clear plexiglass formed the machine structure and enclosure.

02 // X-Y MOTION

Manual positioning

Two user-operated cranks drove a pulley-and-string mechanism to position the claw across the playing field.

03 // Z MOTION

Motorized travel

A 9 V DC motor and winch controlled vertical claw travel, averaging 4.1 s down and 4.3 s up across ten test cycles.

04 // END EFFECTOR

Pneumatic claw

A LEGO hand pump, valve, tubing, and pneumatic claw provided the gripping action; testing averaged 9.6 pumps per successful grab.

Testing also included 100 consecutive structural cycles with no observed bracket cracks or shifts, no screw loosening, and less than 5 mm measured deflection during the structural test. Total project cost was $464.79, including donated/existing materials.
